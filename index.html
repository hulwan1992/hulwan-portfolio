I work where field capture meets delivery — where a scan either becomes something a city can act on, or it doesn’t.

For eight years I’ve moved between the field and the processing desk: planning mobile and terrestrial LiDAR missions, cleaning and validating point clouds, and making sure the final dataset survives contact with a client. Today I lead field data processing at Greehill Asia-Pacific, supporting NParks Singapore’s tree and road-network management at national scale.

That means coordinating field, validation and software teams across Singapore, Malaysia, Brazil, Australia and Europe — with repeatable QC and handovers that hold up after the project doubles in size.
