I work where field capture meets delivery — where a scan either becomes something a city can act on, or it doesn’t.

For eight years I’ve moved between the field and the processing desk: planning mobile and terrestrial LiDAR missions, cleaning and validating point clouds, and making sure the final dataset survives contact with a client. Today I lead field data processing at Greehill Asia-Pacific, supporting NParks Singapore’s tree and road-network management at national scale.

That scope means coordinating field crews, validation and semantic-extraction teams, and software engineers across Singapore, Malaysia, Brazil, Australia and the Europe region — while keeping quality, schedule and handover aligned. I care about the unglamorous parts: repeatable QC, clean metadata, and workflows that don’t break when a project doubles in size.

Before Greehill, I spent five years at YJP Surveyors scanning reclamation sites, refineries, coastlines and heritage buildings — and building the automation that made repetitive volume work faster. That mix of hands-on capture and process design is what I bring to a delivery team.
